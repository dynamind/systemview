# The milk system of systems, told by consumer pull: start at what people
# want on the table, and ask how they get it. The ways to get it compete on
# what they offer, each with its own trade-offs: the residue it leaves, and
# what that hurts. A way can need parts, systems that serve no need of their
# own. Inside a way, one part can answer what another leaves.

need Dairy on the table
  for consumers
  cares about: safe, consistent, affordable, accessible
  uses: milk
  why: People want milk, butter and cheese on the table: safe to eat, the same every time, at a price they can pay, and there when they want it.

# ------------------------------------------------------------ three ways

system Cow and bucket
  serves Dairy on the table with fresh milk, while the cow is in milk
  how: a family keeps a cow and milks it by hand
  why: The first answer. One cow, one bucket, one household.
  leaves: + fresh milk, while the cow is in milk
          dry for weeks before each calf (hurts: accessible)
          spoils in a day (hurts: accessible, safe)
          differs per cow and season (hurts: consistent)
          a cow to buy and keep (hurts: affordable)
          inconvenience: housing, feed, care, calves, manure (hurts: accessible)
          ~ methane

system Farm shop
  serves Dairy on the table with fresh milk, every day
  how: buy milk, butter and cheese at a farm nearby
  leaves: + fresh milk, every day
          + butter and cheese that keep
          a trip to the farm (hurts: accessible)
          every farm makes its own (hurts: consistent)

system Market
  serves Dairy on the table with fresh milk, every day
  how: buy dairy in a shop around the corner, any day
  needs: Dairy farm, Milk collection, Dairy plant
  uses: packaged milk from Dairy plant
  leaves: + fresh milk, every day
          + good, even quality
          + dairy around the corner, any day
          a higher price (hurts: affordable)
          ~ a higher environmental impact
          a long chain to keep running
          ? who sets the price

# ------------------------------------------------------------ what the market needs

system Dairy farm
  how: a farm milks its herd for the dairy, not for its own customers
  why: Selling to a dairy is a different business from selling at the door.
  uses: feed, water, genetics, energy, vet and contractors
  leaves: + raw milk
          + calves and beef
          differs per farm and season (hurts: consistent)
          too little milk to supply a market (hurts: accessible)
          manure
          ~ methane

system Milk collection
  answers too little milk to supply a market
  how: a tanker picks up the milk of many farms
  why: The market needs scale, lots of milk. One farm can't deliver that; many farms together can.
  uses: raw milk from Dairy farm, a driver, fuel
  leaves: + raw milk in bulk
          milk from many farms mixed (hurts: safe, consistent)

system Dairy plant
  answers milk from many farms mixed
  how: a cooperative plant pasteurizes, homogenizes, standardizes and packs the milk of its members
  uses: raw milk in bulk from Milk collection, energy, water
  leaves: + packaged milk
          + safe to drink
          + always the same taste and fat
          + scale lowers the cost per liter

rule Milk quota
  presses on Dairy farm
  why: From 1984 to 2015 the EU capped how much milk each farm could deliver, to end the milk lakes and butter mountains.
